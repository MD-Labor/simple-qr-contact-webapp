Maryland Department of Labor — Internal Applications

Applications

  • Email Signature Generator — build your signature and a contact QR code.

API

The QR generator is a general-purpose endpoint — it encodes any string, not just contact cards.

  • GET /api/qr.png?data=hello — encode any text.
  • GET /api/qr.svg?data=https://maryland.gov — vector output for print.
  • GET /api/mecard.png?first=Jay&last=Huie&email=jay.huie@maryland.gov — build and encode a contact card.
  • GET /api — full parameter reference.
